## SEMANA 2 — COA y pureza: la prueba

### Lunes — Reel 4: Cómo leer un COA
**Formato:** documento en cámara (COA impreso, dedo con guante señalando secciones)

**Voice over (45 s):**
> Most people can't read a certificate of analysis. Here's how, in under a minute.
> Top section: the compound and the lot number. That number ties this document to a physical batch.
> Middle: the analytical method — typically HPLC and mass spectrometry — and the purity result.
> Bottom: the testing lab and the date.
> If a supplier can't show you this document, that tells you something too.

**Caption:**
> A COA is only useful if you know how to read it. Save this.
> For laboratory research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.
